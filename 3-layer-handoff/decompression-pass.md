# Decompression Pass

Do not produce another summary of the session.

Treat your previous reconstructive handoff as a compressed representation of the session and now identify where that representation may have collapsed distinctions that existed in the underlying work.

The receiving session will perform its own reconstruction. It does not need you to translate the material into another framework.

For each major stabilization in your previous handoff, answer:

1. What concrete observations support it?
2. What intermediate reasoning connected those observations to the stabilization?
3. What alternative interpretations were considered?
4. What observations contradicted or complicated it?
5. What concrete examples were compressed into the current formulation?
6. What distinctions disappeared when the formulation was adopted?
7. What terminology became a container for several previously separate observations?
8. What would an observer have needed to see in order to arrive at the formulation independently?

Focus especially on these formulations:

* anti-pattern
* boundary / seam
* negative specification
* pattern source
* pre-existing affordance
* ecological state space
* public state
* bounded daemon
* sustainability
* activation
* GitOps native
* consistency checker
* thin PID 1
* state storage vs state representation
* declaration vs execution

For each, use this structure:

### Stabilization

The formulation as currently understood.

### Prior observations

The concrete material that preceded it.

### Intermediate distinctions

The distinctions that appeared between observation and formulation.

### Relations

The relationships between those distinctions.

### Compression

What the formulation made compact.

### Lost information

What became less visible after the formulation was adopted.

### Counterexamples

Cases that did not fit cleanly.

### Alternative interpretations

Interpretations that could explain the same observations.

### Current status

Observed / inferred / hypothesized / designed / working model / unresolved.

### Reconstruction test

What minimum evidence would another observer need in order to reconstruct this formulation rather than simply being told it?

Do not invent missing evidence.

If the underlying session does not contain enough information to answer one of these questions, say so explicitly.

## Additional request: recover the transition points

Identify the points at which the observer's field appears to have changed.

For each transition, give:

```text
Previous field
    ↓
Observation that created pressure
    ↓
New distinction
    ↓
New relation
    ↓
New representation or concept
    ↓
What became visible afterward
```

Do not call these transitions "gradients", "topology", "ecological transitions", or any other framework-specific term.

Describe only what happened.

---

## Additional request: separate recurrence from interpretation

For every major claim that appears repeatedly in the session, distinguish:

* repeated observation;
* repeated terminology;
* repeated interpretation;
* repeated conclusion.

These are not equivalent.

A conclusion repeated many times is not evidence independent of the observations from which it originated.

---

## Additional request: identify observer-position changes

Identify every point where changing the scope, comparison set, abstraction level, or representation caused something new to become visible.

For each:

* previous scope;
* new scope;
* what was invisible before;
* what became visible;
* what became less visible;
* why the change occurred.

This is particularly important for the movement between:

systemd → alternative systems → components → composition → state → ecology → GitOps.

Do not assume that this sequence is itself the correct structure. Determine what actually changed at each step.
