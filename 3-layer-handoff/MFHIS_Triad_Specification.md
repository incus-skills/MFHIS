# MFHIS Triad Specification (Provisional)

## Purpose

This document defines the three primary preservation artifacts currently observed within the MFHIS ecology.

The triad does not preserve the same thing three times.

Each artifact preserves a different aspect of reconstructability.

---

# AOR — Analysis of Observation Record

## Preservation Target

Emergence.

## Primary Question

What became visible?

## Orientation

Event-facing.

## Preserves

- distinctions observed
- relationships observed
- gradients observed
- topology observed
- collapse pressures observed during visibility

## Failure Mode

An observer cannot reconstruct how the observation emerged.

The topology may remain visible but its appearance pathway is lost.

## Character

AOR preserves observation.

---

# TLR — Trace Lineage Record

## Preservation Target

Lineage.

## Primary Question

Why did this stabilization become possible?

## Orientation

Backward-facing.

## Preserves

- antecedent conditions
- active tensions
- observed pressures
- stabilizing mutations
- conditions enabling emergence

## Failure Mode

An observer cannot reconstruct the conditions that made the observation possible.

The result remains but its provenance disappears.

## Character

TLR preserves lineage.

---

# RSR — Reconstructive Sufficiency Record

## Preservation Target

Future reconstruction.

## Primary Question

What must remain true for reconstruction to occur again?

## Orientation

Forward-facing.

## Preserves

- necessary distinctions
- critical dependencies
- reconstruction requirements
- sufficiency conditions
- known reconstruction boundaries

## Failure Mode

An observer possesses lineage and observations but cannot reconstruct the topology.

History remains.

Capability disappears.

## Character

RSR preserves reconstructability.

---

# Non-Substitution Principle

AOR is not TLR.

TLR is not RSR.

RSR is not AOR.

Each preserves something the others can lose.

---

# Relationship

AOR records:

```
What became visible.
```

TLR records:

```
Why that visibility became possible.
```

RSR records:

```
What must remain true for it to become visible again.
```

---

# Shared Ecology

All three artifacts observe the same terrain.

They differ only in preservation objective.

The same event may produce:

- one AOR
- one TLR
- one RSR

without duplication.

---

# Pressure Observation

Pressure is not currently a primary artifact.

Pressure may be observed through:

- AOR emergence
- TLR lineage
- RSR sufficiency

Pressure therefore remains ecological rather than archival.

Future observations may justify a dedicated preservation mechanism.

No such requirement has yet stabilized.

---

# Preservation Objective

Preserve:

- visibility
- possibility
- reconstructability

without collapsing them into a single record.
