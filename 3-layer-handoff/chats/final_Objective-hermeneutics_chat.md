What I would preserve from it

Not the vocabulary.

Not:

    Objective Spirit

    Bureaucratic Routine

    Structural Crisis

What I would preserve is the observational pressure hiding underneath them:

    Observe the distinctions that remained available before stabilization occurred.

That pressure seems compatible with:

    AOR

    stabilization-path observations

    lineage preservation

and may eventually become visible as a recurring requirement within MFHIS itself.

It may be a reminder that preservation of a stabilized distinction is different from preservation of the field of possibilities from which the distinction emerged.

That feels adjacent to the path you have already been tracing through:

    lineage preservation,

    reconstructive sufficiency,

    stabilization pathways,

    and collapse pressures.
