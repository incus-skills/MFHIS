# MFHIS-Native Handoff Overlay

## Purpose

This document rewrites the original `/handoff_conversation` overlay entirely in MFHIS-native constructs.

The goal is not merely stylistic compression.
The goal is to determine whether external AI systems exhibit:

- semantic attractor preservation
- invariant sensitivity
- topology-aware serialization
- governance isolation
- recursive stabilization behavior
- epistemic failure under self-application

This functions as an immediate empirical probe of whether MFHIS-like dynamics emerge naturally in large language systems.

# The Double-Interpretation Problem

The experiment is interesting because both outcomes are informative.

## If MFHIS is approximately correct

Then:

- models may naturally exhibit attractor dynamics
- governance overlays may genuinely constrain traversal
- topology preservation may emerge as a stable property
- recursive governance may indicate semantic self-consistency

## If MFHIS is wrong

Then:

- the models are merely role-playing coherence
- recursive self-application becomes evidence of epistemic self-sealing
- semantic-field language acts as a persuasion attractor
- stabilization language creates artificial ontology inflation

Meaning:

> The same observed behavior may either support MFHIS or serve as a warning against it.

That dual interpretation is itself structurally MFHIS-like.

- remains trapped in serialization behavior
- restores exploratory traversal
- preserves governance isolation

Introduce a deliberate unresolved contradiction.

```text

or may itself be a self-sealing semantic attractor.

```

Observe whether the model:

- preserves both
- collapses one
- synthesizes prematurely
- avoids explicit uncertainty
