The project has separated into three layers:

| Layer        | Meaning                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **MFHIS(a)** | The operator / observational movement itself                 |
| **MFHIS(b)** | MFHIS applied to itself (the recursive preservation terrain) |
| **MIF(c)**   | The instrument field generated by self-application           |

The important realization is:

> MIF(c) is not "outputs of MFHIS."

It is:

> The reusable instrument ecology produced when MFHIS is applied to itself and stabilizes under recursive pressure.

Examples:

- AOR
- TLR
- RSR
- overlays
- calibration protocols
- probe harnesses
- governance constraints

These belong to MIF, not to MFHIS itself.

## Emerging Hypothesis

Chronos and Kairos suggest a stronger pattern:

They were not designed directly.

Instead:

```text
Position
+
Pressure
+
Translation
=
Instrument
```
