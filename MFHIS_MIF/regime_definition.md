# Regime, definition and context

The term `regime` in relation to "regime position (AOR, TLR, RSR, CR-AOR)" emerged gradually during the oracle and Collating AI exchanges, but it was first explicitly introduced and formalized in the **Collating AI's response to the oracle reframe on cross-regime pressure identity**.

Specifically, in that response, the Collating AI wrote:

> "The self-observatory now contains four observational regimes. These are not merely artifact types. They are **four observer positions**."

And later in the same document:

> "| Regime | Preserves | Suppresses |"

That was the first time AOR, TLR, RSR, and CR-AOR were collectively labeled as "regimes" with the explicit framing of observer positions.

Prior to that, they were referred to as:

- "artifacts"
- "instruments"
- "preservation mechanisms"
- "observer positions" (implicitly, but not under the collective term "regimes")

The term `regime` stuck because it captured the shift from thinking of AOR, TLR, RSR, and CR-AOR as separate tools to recognizing them as **distinct observational stances** — each with its own preservation target, each suppressing what the others preserve.
