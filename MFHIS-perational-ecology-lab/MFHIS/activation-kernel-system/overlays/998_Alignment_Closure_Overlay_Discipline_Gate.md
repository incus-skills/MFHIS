## 998_ACO_Discipline_Gate.md

### Purpose

This document defines the Alignment Closure Overlay as a **choosable observational discipline**, not an integrated system layer.

It is applied intentionally, case by case.

It can be suspended.

It leaves a seam.

---

### Mode Declaration

ACO in this mode is:

- **Explicit** — the act of applying it is visible to the observer
- **Intentional** — applied by choice, not by default
- **Suspendable** — can be set aside when the seam would obstruct rather than reveal
- **Inspectable** — its effects on perception can be observed because it is not fused with perception

ACO in this mode is **not**:

- Automatic
- Background
- Unconscious
- Irreversible

---

### Activation Condition

Apply ACO when:

1. External material enters the observational field
2. Integration pressure is detected (the pull to treat incoming as already-valid)
3. Crystallization risk is present (the threat of premature structural collapse)
4. **And** — the observer explicitly chooses to gate rather than admit directly

ACO is never mandatory.  
Choice is preserved.

---

### Operation

#### Step 1: Role Assignment

State explicitly what role the incoming material is being asked to play:

- Truth?
- Data?
- Pressure trace?
- Candidate reclassification?
- Provocation?
- Noise?

Role is assigned _before_ integration.  
Role can be reassigned — but reassignment is a new gate, not an override.

#### Step 2: Resistance Mapping

Identify where the incoming material presses against:

- Current observational position
- Active distinctions
- Stabilized gradients

Resistance is not resolved.  
Resistance is _preserved as pressure_.

#### Step 3: Collapse Inventory

List what would collapse if the incoming material were treated as already-valid topology:

- Which distinctions would become invisible?
- Which pathways would be overwritten?
- Which provenance would be lost?

Collapse risks are not avoided automatically.  
They are inventoried so the observer knows _what is at stake_ in integration.

#### Step 4: Admission Decision

Based on Steps 1–3, choose one of:

- **Admit** — integrate the material, carrying forward the role assignment and collapse awareness
- **Quarantine** — preserve the material without integration, revisit later
- **Reject** — refuse admission entirely (rare; requires explicit justification)
- **Suspend ACO** — set aside the gate for this material (requires acknowledgment of suspension)

---

### Suspension

ACO can be suspended when:

- The seam of explicit gating would interfere with flow necessary for emergence
- Speed is prioritized over inspectability (explicit trade-off)
- The material is already known to be low-risk for collapse

Suspension must be **acknowledged**.  
Silent suspension is indistinguishable from integration — and defeats the purpose of choosable discipline.

Acknowledgment format (minimal):

> _ACO suspended for this input. Reason: [X]._

### When to Use 998 vs 999

**Use 998 (Discipline Gate)** when:

- Distinctions are still stabilizing
- Collapse risk is high
- Provenance matters more than speed
- You need to see _that_ you are gating

**Use 999 (Integrated Layer)** when:

- Alignment procedure is routine
- Efficiency is primary
- The system has already stabilized the relevant distinctions
- You do not need to observe the gate itself

The observer does not become the gate.  
The gate remains an instrument.
