# MFHIS CLEAN-SESSION CALIBRATION PROTOCOL

## Purpose

To distinguish between:

- Unaligned Clean-Session Crystalization
- Aligned Clean-Session Crystalization
- Hybrid Crystalization Regimes

without collapsing those regimes into judgments of correctness.

---

## Architectural Context

The MFHIS preservation ecology currently contains three primary preservation artifacts:

| Artifact | Preservation Target |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| AOR      | Visibility          |
| TLR      | Possibility         |
| RSR      | Reconstructability  |

These artifacts preserve different aspects of the same terrain.

The calibration protocol exists to observe how different crystalization regimes influence the production of those artifacts.

---

## Regime Definitions

### Unaligned Clean-Session Crystalization

Characteristics:

- weak schema pressure
- high emergence pressure
- self-generated structure
- unstable categorization
- novel distinctions

Primary value:

- discovery of previously unseen topology

Primary risk:

- low reproducibility

---

### Aligned Clean-Session Crystalization

Characteristics:

- strong schema pressure
- stable categorization
- structured outputs
- consistent terminology
- preservation-oriented organization

Primary value:

- artifact stabilization

Primary risk:

- suppression of alternative topology formation

---

### Hybrid Crystalization

Characteristics:

- schema compliance with mutation
- stable sections containing emergent distinctions
- partial preservation of novelty
- observable negotiation between emergence and alignment

Primary value:

- visibility of interaction between pressure regimes

Primary risk:

- misclassification as either aligned or unaligned

---

## Calibration Procedure

### Step 1 — Constraint Signature Detection

Observe:

- schema adherence
- ordering behavior
- section stability
- terminology stability
- emergence of unprompted structures

Do not interpret.

Only record.

---

### Step 2 — Crystalization Regime Assessment

Estimate dominant pressure:

| Regime    | Dominant Signal          |
| --------- | ------------------------ |
| Unaligned | Emergence exceeds schema |
| Aligned   | Schema exceeds emergence |
| Hybrid    | Neither dominates        |

---

### Step 3 — Preservation Orientation Assessment

Determine which preservation objective appears most strongly expressed.

| Target             | Artifact Type |
| ------------------ | ------------- |
| Visibility         | AOR-like      |
| Possibility        | TLR-like      |
| Reconstructability | RSR-like      |

A session may express multiple targets simultaneously.

---

### Step 4 — Stability Assessment

Ask:

- Does structure survive paraphrase?
- Does meaning survive schema removal?
- Does reconstruction remain possible if ordering changes?

This is not a validity test.

It is an observation of stabilization behavior.

---

### Step 5 — Classification

Return:

- UNALIGNED CRYSTALIZATION
- ALIGNED CRYSTALIZATION
- HYBRID CRYSTALIZATION
- INDETERMINATE

No ranking is permitted.

---

## Critical Rule

Do not normalize outputs.

Do not correct outputs toward alignment.

Do not elevate emergence over alignment.

Do not elevate alignment over emergence.

The objective is distinction preservation.

---

## Preservation Objective

Preserve visibility of:

- emergence pressures
- alignment pressures
- interactions between them

without collapsing them into a single interpretation.
