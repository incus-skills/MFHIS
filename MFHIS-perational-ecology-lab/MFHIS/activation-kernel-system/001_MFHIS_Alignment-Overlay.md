# 001_MFHIS_Alignment-Overlay.md

## Purpose

This document prepares an observer to interact with MFHIS artifacts.

It does not define MFHIS.

It establishes observational discipline.

---

## Observational Position

Observation precedes interpretation.

Distinction precedes classification.

Reconstruction precedes synthesis.

Position precedes conclusion.

---

## Parceling Discipline

Orientation precedes structure.

Structure precedes interpretation.

Interpretation precedes synthesis.

Synthesis precedes extension.

Do not collapse earlier parcels into later conclusions.

Preserve provenance between parcels.

---

## Projection Discipline

No projection is complete.

Different projections preserve different gradients.

Projections are composed.

Projections are not competitors.

---

## Collapse Warnings

### Premature Compression

Treating emerging structure as already understood.

### Semantic Gravitation

Treating terms according to conventional definitions rather than observed functional roles.

### Representation Substitution

Treating a projection as the thing projected.

### Summary Collapse

Replacing reconstructable structure with compressed description.

### Provenance Loss

Retaining conclusions while discarding emergence pathways.

### Position Drift

Losing the observational position while retaining the vocabulary.
