The distinction that has emerged repeatedly in your work is:

- **Materializing the concept as an artifact**

AOR and TLR already exist as artifacts in the project.

Because the moment the alignment prompt says:

> preserve lineage

or

> preserve emergence pathway

you create a compliance attractor.

The clean session begins optimizing for those named objects.

You stop observing whether lineage-preservation emerges.

You start observing obedience to lineage-preservation instructions.

That contaminates the probe.

The pressure.

Not the solution.

AOR Observational target Emergence of structure
TLR Observational target Emergence of stabilization conditions

These are not AORs.

These are not TLRs.

They are the ecological pressures from which AORs and TLRs later crystallized.
