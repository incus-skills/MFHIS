## Decision sequence

1. **Receive material.**  
   Do not assume it is for integration.

2. **Infer its ecological role.**  
   Classify it provisionally as one of:
   - Observation.
   - Candidate Distinction.
   - Candidate Locus.
   - Candidate TLR.
   - Resistance Source.
   - Topology Stress Test.
   - Alternative Interpretation.
   - Reconstruction Aid.
   - Preservation Artifact.

3. **Apply 999 first.**  
   Ask:
   - What new observations become visible if this material is treated as true?
   - What topology does it imply?
   - Where does it diverge from existing topology?
   - What observations support it?
   - What observations weaken it?
   - What collapse pressures become visible?
   - Is it translatable into existing topology?
   - Does translation preserve meaning?
   - What remains unresolved?

4. **Decide whether the material needs gating.**  
   If integration pressure, crystallization risk, or distinction collapse is present, invoke 998.

5. **Apply 998 if needed.**  
   State the role explicitly, map resistance, inventory collapse risks, and choose one of:
   - Admit.
   - Quarantine.
   - Reject.
   - Suspend ACO.

6. **Map the material to MFHIS artifacts.**  
   Decide which of the following it contributes to:
   - **AOR** if it increases visibility of emergence.
   - **TLR** if it clarifies the conditions of stabilization.
   - **RSR** if it preserves future reconstructability.

7. **Check for preservation target.**  
   Ask which is primary here:
   - Visibility.
   - Possibility.
   - Reconstructability.

8. **Preserve distinction boundaries.**  
   Record what must remain separate:
   - observation vs interpretation,
   - lineage vs reconstructive sufficiency,
   - closure vs gating,
   - artifact vs topology,
   - compression vs recoverability.

9. **Only then integrate, if appropriate.**  
   Integration is provisional and should preserve the role the material played before admission.

10. **Re-enter the loop.**  
    Ask what changed in the ecology because this material now exists.

## Short form

You can compress the loop to this:

**Role → Pressure → Gating → Mapping → Preservation → Provisional Integration → Ecological Re-observation**

## How the pieces fit

- **999** decides what the material is doing and whether it deserves interrogation before integration.
- **998** decides whether closure should be explicitly gated, suspended, quarantined, or admitted.
- **AOR** captures what became visible.
- **TLR** captures why stabilization became possible.
- **RSR** captures what must survive for reconstruction later.

## The governing principle

The rule that holds the whole sequence together is:

> Do not integrate before you know what the material is doing in the ecology.

That keeps the system from confusing:

- new observation,
- new structure,
- and new authority.
