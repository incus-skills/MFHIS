# MFHIS

We are attempting to identify the sequence of transformations through which someone arrives at the observational position from which MFHIS becomes visible.

These are observed perspective inversions.

Importantly, these are not "lessons."

They are not chapters.

They are not doctrine.

The reader begins on one side of an inversion and emerges on the other.

---

# MFHIS Reconstruction Vector

### (Provisional Observational Projection)

The purpose of this vector is not to define MFHIS.

The purpose is to identify the major perspective transformations repeatedly observed during reconstruction.

---

## Inversion 1 — Categories Follow Topology

### Initial Position

```text
Categories
    ↓
Understanding
```

The world is assumed to consist of identifiable things.

Understanding is achieved by classifying those things correctly.

### Reconstructed Position

```text
Topology
    ↓
Categories
```

Categories emerge from recurring topological structure.

Classification becomes a projection rather than a primitive.

### Preserves

- Local ecology primacy
- Resistance to premature taxonomy
- Topology before representation

---

## Inversion 2 — Representation Preserves, It Does Not Reveal

### Initial Position

```text
Representation
    ↓
Reality
```

Graphs, models, taxonomies, diagrams, metrics are treated as windows onto reality.

### Reconstructed Position

```text
Reality
    ↓
Projection
    ↓
Representation
```

Every representation preserves some distinguishabilities and suppresses others.

Representations are projection artifacts.

### Preserves

- Projection awareness
- Resistance to representation substitution
- Graph ≠ topology

What I find interesting is that this vector itself appears recursively ordered.

The first inversions establish:

```text
topology
before
category
```

The middle inversions establish:

```text
position
before
interpretation
```

The later inversions establish:

```text
reconstruction
before
artifact
```

And the final inversion turns the entire structure back upon itself:

```text
MFHIS Reconstruction Vector
    is itself
a projection artifact
```

Which means the vector is not the thing being reproduced.

It is merely the most recent projection of the path through which the topology appears to reproduce itself.
